# 📊 HR Attrition Analysis Dashboard

## 📌 Overview
This project analyzes employee attrition at **Accenture** using HR data. The dashboard provides insights into why employees leave the company and highlights key factors influencing retention.  

The goal is to help HR leaders make **data‑driven decisions** to reduce turnover and improve employee engagement.

---

## 🚀 Features
- **KPI Cards**: Total Employees, Attrition Count, Attrition %  
- **Department Analysis**: Attrition % by Department (R&D, Sales, HR, etc.)  
- **Demographics**: Attrition by Age, Gender, Education, Tenure  
- **Factors Influencing Attrition**:  
  - Work‑Life Balance  
  - Job Satisfaction  
  - Overtime  
  - Monthly Income & Salary Hike  
- **Performance & Retention**: Attrition trends by performance rating and education level  

---

## 🛠️ Tech Stack
- **Power BI** → Dashboard creation & visualization  
- **DAX** → Custom measures (Attrition %, Avg Income, etc.)  
- **Dataset** → HR Employee Attrition dataset (CSV/Excel)  

---

## 📈 Key Insights
- Attrition is highest in the **Research & Development** department.  
- **Work‑Life Balance** and **Job Satisfaction** are stronger predictors of attrition than salary hikes.  
- Employees working **overtime** show higher attrition rates.  
- **Monthly income** has little impact on attrition.
